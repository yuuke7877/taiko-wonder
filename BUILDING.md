`SDL2main.lib` is intentionally not linked in the Visual Studio project. `src/sdl_main_compat.h` defines `SDL_MAIN_HANDLED` and calls `SDL_SetMainReady()` before `main()`, so the executable uses SDL2's regular console entry point and does not require the optional SDL2main wrapper library. SDL2 and SDL2_ttf themselves are still supplied by the vcpkg manifest.
